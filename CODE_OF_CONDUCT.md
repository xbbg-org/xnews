# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project is a small technical community. We expect everyone participating in it —
issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews — to be respectful and constructive.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Give and accept technical criticism on the merits, with evidence.
- Assume good faith and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone officially representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **alpha.xone@outlook.com**. Reports are reviewed
confidentially and investigated promptly.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not meet this standard, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
